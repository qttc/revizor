Acme 2.4 launch recap

In today's fast-paced app market, the 2.4 release marks a pivotal moment for Acme — it's not just an update, it's a whole new way to build habits.

What shipped

  • Streak freeze: one missed day per week no longer resets a streak.
  • Home screen widgets for the three most-used habits.
  • Sync between phone and watch now takes under two seconds.
